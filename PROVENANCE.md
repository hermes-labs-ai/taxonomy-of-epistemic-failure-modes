# Provenance

## Canonical published artifact

The canonical public artifact is the PDF attached to Zenodo record
`19042469`, DOI <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19042469>.

- Zenodo filename:
  `A Taxonomy of Epistemic Failure Modes in Large Language Models - Bosch 2026.pdf`
- Zenodo MD5: `d4e26daf91a6520b9684a925c3fe2c11`
- Repository filename: `taxonomy-epistemic-failure-modes.pdf`
- Repository SHA-256:
  `816e27b1748f4b3dd9243fc45a942d8c3c29eb525a88f7efad9398f7e77f6dc8`

The repository PDF is byte-identical to the Zenodo file. It has not been
re-rendered, optimized, or modified.

## Source recovery boundary

Editable manuscript and appendix candidates were recovered locally from the
author repository `roli-lpci/zenodo-papers` at commit
`3224b2f4f51ba66f8df7d58067e6a83a5ed8366c`.

Those files are not included in this minimum public package. They were not
proven to reproduce the deposited PDF bytes, and review found material that
requires reconciliation against the published paper and underlying records
before a separate source release. Their omission does not alter the canonical
PDF and does not imply that editable source never existed.

The published PDF remains authoritative. Any later source release must preserve
the recovered bytes, identify their exact source commit and checksums, document
material differences from the deposited paper, and use a separate release
decision.
