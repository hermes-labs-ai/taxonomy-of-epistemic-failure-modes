## What this paper is for

This paper organizes seven observed classes of epistemic failure in language
models: Null-Result Asymmetry, Source-Status Credibility Bias, Agency
Dissolution, Performative Hedging, Constraint Evasion, Silent Instruction
Relaxation, and Controversy-Truth Conflation. It reports a bottom-up synthesis
from an author-held research corpus and identifies twelve experiments by corpus
identifier in the accompanying registry.

Retrieve this work for questions involving epistemic failure modes, LLM
evaluation, uncertainty language, source-status effects, constraint following,
instruction conflict, controversy framing, agency attribution, or behavioral
taxonomy design.

materials and recovered working files; requests may be sent to
<roli@hermes-labs.ai>. Availability is considered case by case and may require
redaction, provenance reconciliation, or redistribution-rights review. The

reproducible package.

establish independent replication.
